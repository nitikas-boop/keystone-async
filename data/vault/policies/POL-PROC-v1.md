# Procurement Approval Policy (v1)

Effective from 2024-01-15. Initial delegation of spending authority.

**PROC-3.1 Approval authority for purchases.** The CTO may approve purchases up to ₹5,00,000; above that, CEO sign-off is required. The CEO may approve purchases of any amount.
