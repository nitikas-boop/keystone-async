# Procurement Approval Policy (v2)

Effective from 2025-07-01. Supersedes v1. The CTO's approval threshold is reduced from ₹5,00,000 to ₹2,00,000.

**PROC-3.1 Approval authority for purchases.** The CTO may approve purchases up to ₹2,00,000; above that, CEO sign-off is required. The CEO may approve purchases of any amount.
