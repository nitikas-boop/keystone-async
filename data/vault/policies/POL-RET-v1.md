# Customer Data Retention Policy (v1)

Effective from 2024-01-15. Initial policy adopted at incorporation.

**RET-2.1 Customer log retention.** Customer activity logs shall be retained for no longer than 365 days.

**RET-4.2 Purpose limitation.** Personal data may be retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
