# Customer Data Retention Policy (v2)

Effective from 2025-01-06. Supersedes v1. The customer log retention cap is reduced from 365 to 180 days ahead of India's data-protection rules.

**RET-2.1 Customer log retention.** Customer activity logs shall be retained for no longer than 180 days.

**RET-4.2 Purpose limitation.** Personal data may be retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
