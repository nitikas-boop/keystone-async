# Customer log retention review

Attendees: Ananya Rao (CEO), Farhan Qureshi (Compliance lead), Vikram Shah (CTO), Sneha Iyer.

Farhan pointed out that the retention policy changed in January: RET-2.1 now caps customer activity logs at 180 days, but we are still keeping them for 300 days under the old arrangement.

Sneha said fraud investigations regularly need five to six months of history, so anything much shorter than 180 days would hurt the analysts.

Vikram proposed 90 days to be safe. Farhan said 180 days is compliant as long as the purge job actually runs.

Ananya decided to reduce customer log retention to 180 days, the longest period the policy allows, because fraud investigations need the longest window available. The decision will go in the log next week.

Action: Vikram to change the purge job from 300 to 180 days.
