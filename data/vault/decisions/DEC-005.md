---
doc_type: decision
decision_id: DEC-005
title: Nightly encrypted backups
decided_on: 2025-04-02
owner: p-divya
project: Internal Ops
visibility: org
status: active
effect: ongoing
reasons: >-
  A restore drill showed the weekly snapshot lost too much data; nightly encrypted backups bound the loss
  to one day and keep backup contents unreadable if the storage is compromised.
---
Divya moved the production databases to nightly encrypted backups, replacing weekly unencrypted snapshots.
