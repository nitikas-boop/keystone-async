decision_id: DEC-002
title: Retain customer logs for 300 days
decided_on: 2024-07-22
owner: p-ananya
relied_on: [RET-2.1]
status: superseded
effect: ongoing
fields: {retention_days: 300}
reasons: >-
  Fraud investigations and chargeback disputes can surface up to ten months after a transaction, and
  the retention policy then in force allowed up to 365 days.
---
Ananya set customer activity log retention at 300 days, inside the 365-day cap of RET-2.1 (v1). This was
later replaced by DEC-007.
