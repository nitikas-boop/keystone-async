# Customer Data Retention Policy (v3)

Effective from 2026-09-28. Supersedes v2. The customer log retention cap is reduced from 180 to 90 days.

**RET-2.1 Customer log retention.** Customer activity logs shall be retained for no longer than 90 days.

**RET-4.2 Purpose limitation.** Personal data may be retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
